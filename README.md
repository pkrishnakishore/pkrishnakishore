# Krishna Kishore Pandalaneni

**Comparative politics · Political institutions · Evidence synthesis**

I am a Columbia SIPA MPA graduate (2025) with research experience spanning UN evaluation, academic evidence synthesis, and industrial R&D.

My developing doctoral research asks: **When do political consultants shift decision-making authority within parties, and toward whom?** I am interested in party organization, grassroots cadres, political expertise, and representation, using India as a setting for comparative inquiry.
